Common questions

Before you call

How fast can someone get to me?

Most Houston-area requests get a truck on site within 25–40 minutes, depending on traffic and your exact location. We'll always give you a real estimate on the call-back, not a guess.

Do you give a price before the tow starts?

Yes. Our dispatcher confirms pricing with you on the phone before any truck is sent, so there are no surprises when the job is done.

What if I'm not sure exactly where I am?

That's fine — give us your closest cross streets, a landmark, or drop a pin from your phone's maps app and read us the coordinates. We'll find you.

Do you tow motorcycles and larger vehicles too?

Yes, light-duty towing covers most cars, trucks, SUVs, and motorcycles. Let us know your vehicle type when you call so we send the right equipment.
